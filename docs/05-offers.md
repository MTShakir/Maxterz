## 0. How the pricing works

**Position:** agency quality at about half of typical UK agency rates, well above freelancer and Fiverr prices. These are proof-building prices for the first year. They rise as case studies and demand grow (rule 4 in section 9).

**Three layers, one logic:**
1. **Service prices.** Every service has a published price: tiers, a "from" price or a fixed price.
2. **Fixed packages (one-time).** 3 packages, each 40 to 47% below the same services bought separately.
3. **Monthly plans.** 3 plans, about 43% below the same services bought separately, plus Care Plus on every website.

**Rules that hold the system together:**
- Prices of £100 and over end in 95. Prices under £100 are round numbers (£30, £50, £75, £95).
- Prices are in GBP. No VAT is added (Maxterz is not VAT registered). International clients pay in GBP by card.
- "Save £X" always uses our own published service prices (CAP Code and the DMCC Act 2024: reference prices must be genuine).
- Package values and savings are calculated from the service prices of their parts, never typed by hand.
- Target gross margin: 60% or more. If delivery time on a package grows past budget, raise the price or cut the scope.
- Out-of-scope work: written change request and quote before work starts. Rate: £45 per hour.

**Payment terms:**
- Projects under £4,000: 50% to start, 50% before launch.
- Projects of £4,000 and over: 40% to start, 30% at design sign-off, 30% before launch.
- Monthly plans: billed monthly in advance by card or Direct Debit. Pay 12 months upfront and get 2 months free.

**Showing prices (D27):** fixed work shows its price. Custom work (apps, software, AI builds, large sites) shows "from £X, quoted after a free call". Every price card leads to "Book a free call", except the 4 self-serve "Buy now" items in section 2.8.

## 1. Who we sell to first

**Primary target for ads and outreach:** small UK service businesses that are new, or established but look dated online. Trades, cleaners, clinics, salons, restaurants, removals, property services, driving schools, local professional services.

**Secondary (inbound):** growing companies, startups and creators who need branding, animation, video editing, apps or monthly marketing. They find us through Instagram, Fiverr, referrals and SEO.

## 9. Pricing rules

1. Publish every fixed price. Custom work shows "from" prices.
2. Never discount the published price in a sales call. Add a bonus instead.
3. Fiverr prices stay on Fiverr. Direct clients pay these prices.
4. Raise prices by 10 to 20% after every 10 sales of a service, or when more than 4 in 10 qualified calls close in a month.
5. Every project ends with an offer for a monthly plan.
6. Review the price book every quarter. Change prices in Supabase, then update this file and `pricing.json`.
