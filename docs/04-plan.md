## 1. The goal and the maths

**North star:** £100,000 revenue in the next 12 months with fixed costs under £200 a month (before ads).

| Milestone | Target | By |
|---|---|---|
| Website live on maxterz.com (maxterz.co.uk redirecting) | Launched, tracking verified | Wed 7 Oct 2026 |
| First agency client (outside Fiverr) | 1 signed deal | Sat 31 Oct 2026 |
| Month 2 | £6,000 cash collected | 30 Nov 2026 |
| Month 3 | £8,000 cash collected in the month, including £1,500 monthly recurring (MRR) | 31 Dec 2026 |
| Month 6 | £10,000 a month, £4,000 of it MRR | 31 Mar 2027 |

**What £8,000 a month looks like:**
- 3 Launch Kits (£1,995) and 2 Starter Kits (£795) = about £7,575
- Monthly clients: 5 Care Plus (£145) and 2 Visibility Plans (£795) = about £2,315, growing every month
- Total: about £9,900 in a realistic month (price book section 6)
- Plus Fiverr income on top

**The funnel that produces it (monthly):**
- 40 qualified leads
- 16 calls booked (40%)
- 12 calls held (75% show rate)
- 3 to 4 deals closed (25 to 33% close rate)

Lead sources: cold outreach 15 to 20, Instagram and LinkedIn 5 to 10, website SEO 5, paid ads 8 to 12, referrals and past clients 3 to 5.

These are targets, not promises. The weekly scorecard (section 7) tells us early if a source underperforms.

## 5. Social media system (starts week 2)

**Goal:** trust and inbound calls, not vanity numbers.

| Platform | Role | Cadence |
|---|---|---|
| Instagram @maxterzhub | Main channel. Proof and demos | 5 reels a week, stories daily |
| LinkedIn (Talha's personal profile + company page) | B2B trust and conversations with owners | 3 text or carousel posts a week, 20 comments a day on target owners' posts |
| TikTok, YouTube Shorts, Facebook | Reach. Repurpose the same reels | Same 5 reels a week |

**Content pillars:**
1. **Proof:** logo animation reveals (your 1.2M-view format), before and after website redesigns, case study clips
2. **Teardowns:** "I fixed this local business website in 60 seconds", "3 reasons this site loses calls"
3. **AI demos:** real AI receptionist calls, "what happens when you miss a call at 9pm"
4. **Founder story:** Fiverr to agency, 10,000+ projects, behind the scenes with the team

**Workflow:** film all reels on Monday, team edits Tuesday and Wednesday, schedule Thursday for the next week. Every post ends with one call to action: "Comment AUDIT for a free website audit video" or "Book a free call, link in bio".

**Instagram profile fixes:** bio states what you do and for whom, link goes to /book, highlights for Work, Reviews, AI Demo and Prices.
