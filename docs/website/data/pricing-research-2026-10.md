## Contract terms seen in the market
- Care plans, Google profile, chatbots: rolling monthly.
- Social, PPC and local SEO: often a 3-month minimum.
- National SEO and full retainers: 6 to 12 months is common, but UK buyer guides now warn against long lock-ins.
- Notice: 30 days, billed monthly in advance. Annual prepay discounts of 10 to 20% are normal.
- Top reason clients leave an agency: unhappy with delivery (48% in one US study). Churn is lowest on full-service retainers.
