### Rules for every claim (UK law: DMCC Act 2024 and the ASA CAP Code)

1. Always say reviews are on Fiverr. Never present Fiverr reviews as direct agency reviews.
2. Use one rating everywhere: 4.9. The homepage currently says 4.8. Fix it.
3. Remove unverified numbers from the current About page: "500+ projects", "350+ happy clients", "5+ years", "98% satisfaction". They conflict with the real figures and cannot be backed up.
4. No invented testimonials, stock "client" photos or fake logos.
5. Results in case studies must be real numbers from the client, or described as deliverables.
